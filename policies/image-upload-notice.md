# Image upload notice

Upload an image only if you are entitled to submit it for the requested
processing. You do not need to claim ownership if you have another
applicable permission or legal basis.

Grimlore uses the image to produce the text analysis or description you
request. Uploading does not transfer your copyright or grant permission
to publish, redistribute, or use the image for unrelated purposes.

Before upload, the application must display:
- The processors that will receive the image.
- Whether OCR, moderation, or image transformation is involved.
- Project-controlled retention and deletion periods.
- Known provider-side retention and deletion limitations.
- Any cross-border processing information required for the deployment.
- The contact or control for privacy and rights requests.

Project image retention, public sharing, evaluation use, and training use
are not enabled merely by submitting a request.

Avoid uploading sensitive information that the task does not need.
People depicted in an image may have privacy or other rights separate
from the uploader's copyright permissions.

The application must not claim that provider-held data has been deleted
unless the relevant provider confirms deletion or its documented
deletion process has completed.
